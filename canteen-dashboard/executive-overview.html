What it tracks

Price history — per item, over time

Category spend — Vegetables, Dairy, Dry goods…
Supplier performance — volume and cost
Monthly trends — month-on-month comparisons

Internal Operations Tool
📊
Live Dashboard
Real-time KPIs — monthly spend, purchase count, average order value, top vendor and top item — updated the moment a new invoice is entered.
🧾
Invoice Entry & Scan
Two entry modes: tabular batch entry for fast keyboard-driven input, or scan-and-annotate with an invoice image attached for reference.
📈
Price Trend Alerts
Tracks rate history for every item. Automatically flags price spikes (>10% rise) and savings (>10% drop) at the point of entry — before you pay.
🏪
Supplier Analytics
Ranks vendors by total spend, order volume, and last activity. Spot over-reliance or under-utilised suppliers at a glance.
📅
Monthly Reports
Item-level summary with total qty, average rate, total spend, and a Stable / Rising / Falling trend indicator. Filter by any date range.
⬇️
Excel Export & Audit
One-click export to .xlsx for accounting. Full audit log records every create/edit/delete with user, timestamp, and changed fields.
